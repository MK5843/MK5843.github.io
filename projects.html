SiteGiant POS Device Scanning Test Issue

Title/Role:
IT/E-Commerce Lead & Shopify Expert · Team Collaboration

The Problem:
◦ Some Purpose Performance Wear and Simply Active products did not automatically add to the cart when scanned using an iOS device in SiteGiant POS, while Android devices could automatically add scanned products to the cart.

Overview:
◦ Collaborated with Store Manager and tested the product scanning process across Android, iOS/iPad and the POS scanner to compare how each device handled scanned products.

What I did:
◦ Compared the scanning and add-to-cart behaviour between devices.
◦ Confirmed that Android could automatically add scanned products to the cart.
◦ Found that iOS required an additional tap to add some scanned products.
◦ Tested the POS scanner and compared its behaviour with the mobile devices.
◦ Investigated products that could not be scanned and found a missing UPC for one product.
◦ Documented the findings and shared the results with the team.

Outcome:
◦ Concluded that the scanning differences were due to device behaviour across Android, iOS/iPad, and the POS scanner, as well as a product UPC issue, rather than a system issue.
